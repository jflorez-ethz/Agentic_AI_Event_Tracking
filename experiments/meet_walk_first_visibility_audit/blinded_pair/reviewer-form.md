# Independent two-frame review

Open before.png and after.png. Review them independently of other reviewers and without the investigation report or trace. For each distinguishable person in the later frame, mark their region and the corresponding region in the earlier frame. For each frame record absent, discernible, or indeterminate. Use indeterminate if a shape is ambiguous or correspondence is unclear.

For each person, record the strongest finding the pair supports: first visibility, movement within view, increased visibility, or indeterminate. Mark any uncertainty about correspondence. Submit your notes before seeing the surrounding source frames. After reviewing the sequence, record any changed judgment separately. Compare reviewers only after both have submitted.
