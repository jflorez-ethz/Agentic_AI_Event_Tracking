# Independent trajectory review

Receive only review-sequence.mkv and this form. Do not inspect the source filename, investigation, or other reviewers' answers. Clip time zero maps to source time 10 s. Playback is 25 fps.

Mark the two visibly distinguishable figures only while you can maintain continuity. Record the last frame at which both trajectories are individually traceable. Label intervals as common direction, observable divergence, obscured or out of view, or indeterminate. Provide frame brackets and visible landmarks. A disappearance behind a foreground feature cannot establish a direction of departure. If a figure reappears, say whether continuity is visible or indeterminate. Submit independently. Report disagreements before adjudication.

For the live A/B follow-up, score the ten common samples (14.52–19.04 s) first, then reveal the ten additional samples (19.52–24.04 s), then the consecutive source. Record separate judgments for each stage. Distinguish the visible loss of a previously traceable person from inability to establish which person is visible. A single movement is not a two-person trajectory.
