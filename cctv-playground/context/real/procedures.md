# Site procedures (demo)

1. The site is closed all day on Sundays. A person or vehicle anywhere on site is expected only if the roster lists them for that zone, or a valid badge was used at a door in that zone in the previous 10 minutes.
2. Unexpected activity while the site is closed: alert the control room with the clip and the neighbouring cameras. Priority high. The guard on duty decides on dispatch; the system never contacts the police by itself.
3. Person down, fire or smoke: always alert, priority high, whatever the time and whoever it is. Never dismissed automatically.
4. Emergency exit obstructed: alert the supervisor, priority medium, to be cleared before the next shift.
5. Do not identify people. Describe what happened, where and when.
6. Recordings are kept for 72 hours unless attached to an incident.
